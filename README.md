# Quality Compressor

A mod for Factorio 2.1 with Space Age and Quality. Compresses identical raw materials of lower quality into one item of the next quality tier: a predictable alternative to recycler loops, paid for with a lot of power.

**Download:** [Factorio Mod Portal](https://mods.factorio.com/mod/quality-compressor) or the in-game mod manager.

Bugs and ideas: open an issue here or post in the Mod Portal discussion.
