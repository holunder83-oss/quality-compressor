Version: 1.0.2
Date: 2026-11-04
  Features:
    - New startup settings "Ratio: <tier>": set the ratio of every target tier (default 8 / 8 / 10 / 12, allowed 2 to 1000), e.g. 40:1 for Legendary to play like Steeper Quality.
    - New startup settings "Ratio factor: <raw material>": multiplies the ratio of all tiers for one raw material (default 1, scrap 2). Result is rounded up, at least 2.

Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-11-02
  Info:
    - First public release (beta). Balancing is still being tuned, feedback welcome.
  Features:
    - Compressor: turns identical raw materials into one item of the next quality tier, up to Rare.
    - High-pressure compressor: compresses up to Legendary, needs materials from Aquilo.
    - Ratios 8:1 (Uncommon, Rare), 10:1 (Epic), 12:1 (Legendary). No randomness, no productivity.
    - Power depends on the target tier (5 / 20 / 80 / 300 MW). Better quality machines use less power.
    - Quality modules work like in the base game, capped by the highest tier of the machine.
    - Raw materials of every planet: iron, copper, stone, coal, uranium, tungsten, calcite, sulfur, holmium plate, scrap, ice. Plastic is compressed in the biochamber and costs nutrients instead of power.
    - One research per raw material (with the science pack of its planet) and per target tier.
    - Works on space platforms and with Nomad.
    - Graphics from CMA Graphics by trentmkelly (CC0).
